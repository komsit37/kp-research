The compounding loop

A conversation becomes a better starting point

Stable learning moves from a one-off interaction into indexed memory, a reusable workflow, or an automated trigger.

01 / RECALLLoad context

Retrieve only the notes, preferences, and prior decisions relevant now.

02 / WORKInvestigate

Read, browse, calculate, code, compare, test, or operate the live system.

03 / DELIVERCreate an outcome

A decision, report, checklist, draft action, fix, or monitoring result.

04 / CURATEKeep what matters

Store durable state; promote repeated learning to knowledge or a playbook.

05 / COMPOUNDReuse or automate

The next session starts ahead—or the system watches without being asked.

“The unit of progress is not a conversation. It is a better starting point next time.”

Brain combines a knowledge base, research workspace, task log, tool layer, and automation registry. The model can change; the accumulated operating context remains.
